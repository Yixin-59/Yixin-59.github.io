I am an undergraduate student majoring in Microelectronics Science and Engineering at the College of Information Science and Electronic Engineering, Zhejiang University.

I have been selected for the academic research track of the **Shannon Honors Program** within the college, and am advised by **Professor Xiaopeng Yu** from the School of Integrated Circuits.

My research interests include Analog EDA and RFIC inverse design. My ongoing work focuses on the design and optimization of pixelated RFICs, as well as research into analog integrated circuit design agents.

For my future academic path, I aim to pursue a **direct PhD program** in analog integrated circuit design, with a specialized focus on high-speed, high-precision ADC design.
